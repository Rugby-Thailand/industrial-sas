## Company setup and supported bounds

HR must review the preset and real company data before activating leave or announcements. Payslips additionally require backend `HR_PAYSLIP_PROVIDER_FRESHNESS_ENABLED` and `HR_PAYSLIP_PRIVATE_STORAGE_ENABLED` capabilities; HR cannot turn those on through a checkbox. Default provider verification is five minutes; supported settings are 2–15 minutes and private download grants 1–60 seconds. SHA-256 checks remain mandatory for stored file integrity even when HR makes supplied import hashes optional. A matching hash proves unchanged bytes, not correct payroll calculations.

Leave uses whole days and refuses a request spanning entitlement periods; the employee splits it into separate requests. The pilot bounds policy/recipient setup to 500 employees and refuses oversized fanout rather than truncating it. Unread counts explicitly identify incomplete counts above 500. Payslip imports support canonical unquoted CSV with at most 20 source rows, small PDFs up to 384 KiB each, and up to 4 MiB aggregate base64. It does not calculate payroll or perform OCR. Original CSV, excluded-row reasons and source PDFs remain private tenant records.
