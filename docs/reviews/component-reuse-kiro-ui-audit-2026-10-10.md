## Scope and method

  `output/component-reuse-ux-2026-10-10/initial-manifest.json`. Images are in
  `output/component-reuse-ux-2026-10-10/initial/` (1440×900 desktop, 390×844 mobile, `/th`,
  dark unless the name contains `light`). Chronology from the manifest's ISO `capturedAt`
  values (73 entries): 2026-10-10T17:37:46.687Z to 2026-10-10T17:52:12.705Z, which is
  **2026-10-11 00:37–00:52 +07 (Bangkok)**, after local midnight. The 8 dark storage captures

  have no `capturedAt`; their file times are 2026-10-11 00:40:34–00:41:16 +07, inside the

  capture date.
  The set is 64 route captures, 8 light-theme specimens and 9 supplemental captures (ticket fields,
  employee fields, guard overlay and global search).
- Design-sheet references: `../evidence/sheet/sheet-{catalogue,scan,floor,hr}-{desktop,mobile}.png`.

## Coordinator corrections to worker findings

- The HR worker reported `HR-EMP-1 mono codes`, but employee codes render in sans. Pixels show
  `EMP-DEMO-001` (Today) and `EMP-DEMO-002 · LOCAL-DEMO` (Review queue) in sans, and source
  confirms it: `ReviewScreen.tsx:347`, `EmployeesScreen.tsx:198,345`, `TodayScreen.tsx:163`
  and `ProfileScreen.tsx:70` are plain text. Finished-goods codes do use mono. See X-4.
- The HR worker read the Today `วันไม่ทำงาน` pill and the `วันนี้ไม่มีกะ…` row as checkboxes.
  They are a neutral `StatusBadge` and a neutral `Notice`. Both draw lucide `Square`
  (`StatusBadge.tsx:30`, `TONE_ICONS.neutral`), and that outline is why they look like
  unchecked checkboxes. This was introduced by the migration: the baseline used a filled `■`
  and Notice had no icon. See X-2.
- The FG worker described `JT-1` as an unverified required-field tint. Source settles it:
  `TicketCard.tsx:78-79` sets `aria-invalid` on any empty required field, so an untouched ticket
  shows the `aria-invalid` red border and ring. The baseline had the same rule, so this is
  pre-existing.
