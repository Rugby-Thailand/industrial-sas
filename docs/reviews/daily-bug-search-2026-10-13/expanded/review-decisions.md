# Independent review of Kiro candidates

This is the consolidation decision for the research, rather than an unreviewed union of every failing diagnostic assertion. Kiro's raw report and logs remain available in `kiro/`. A failing assertion proves the observed behavior; the intended behavior must also be supported by the product contract.

## Include as distinct bugs

| ID      | Decision                                                     | Evidence and boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| KIRO-03 | Include: self-review control bypass                          | HR-001, HR-032 and HR-033 explicitly prohibit reviewing one's own attendance. The real authorized handlers reject direct self-review, then accept unlink → certify → relink, and the certification actor is the same user. This is a trusted HR administrator bypass, not an ordinary employee privilege escalation.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| KIRO-05 | Include: historical records consume the current-period bound | `siteEmployeesInRange` counts all site employee records before applying employment dates. The fixture has two people employed in the requested range and 100 former workers, but returns PERIOD_TOO_LARGE/EMPLOYEES and LIMIT_EXCEEDED. The current range is below the declared 50-person limit. This concerns lifetime directory growth, not an objection to the explicit 50-person/31-day pilot bounds. The fixture inserts the historical records directly; setup has no corresponding lifetime creation guard. |
| KIRO-07 | Include, lower priority: unchanged time side is altered      | The real correction prefill converts 08:30:45 to 08:30, and submit sends that side even when the employee only changes the other side. The actual backend stores proposedStartAt 45 seconds earlier. Original events remain immutable and minute precision for an explicitly edited field is supported; the problem is treating an untouched original side as edited without making the change visible.                                                                                                            |
| KIRO-10 | Include: reactive version remount destroys dirty draft       | The actual employee editor is keyed by employee ID plus version. A query update from another administrator remounts the form, replacing a typed unsaved name with its initial value without a draft prompt. Backend expectedVersion is present, so this is client draft loss rather than an undetected server lost update.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| KIRO-11 | Include, lower priority: incomplete period work list         | The actual UI renders 200 of 217 unresolved rows and omits the final missing-record exception without a truncation notice. Totals and server close checks remain correct; the separate Review entry is a workaround. Count this as incomplete task visibility, not a false-success close or truncated calculation.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Keep out of the confirmed-bug count

| ID      | Status                                                 | Observed behavior                                                                                                                                         | Why it needs a policy decision or is deliberate behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| KIRO-01 | Data-impact risk requiring policy confirmation         | Changing employment dates to exclude an already-recorded day omits that employee's time from a newly closed period/CSV; original attendance stays stored. | HR-040 says to include people employed in the range. The new employment dates can be an intentional correction of an invalid earlier registry entry. The current spec does not explicitly say whether recorded days outside corrected employment dates must block closing. A warning or conflict is a defensible improvement, but do not label the observed selection rule a confirmed P1 without that decision. This is attendance CSV, not a payroll calculation. |
| KIRO-02 | Historical schedule risk requiring policy confirmation | Changing a live schedule or adding a retrospective holiday changes missing-day exceptions before a period is closed.                                      | HR-012 explicitly freezes the plan for recorded days; HR-013 freezes historical closed output. Unrecorded days in open periods use the live plan. There is no effective-dated schedule engine in the pilot requirements, and a legitimate late-entered holiday should remove absence. A future-effective schedule change is a reasonable product need, but the probe assumes that policy.                                                                           |
| KIRO-04 | Access-policy question                                 | An HR_ADMIN member scoped to site A can revoke HR_SUPERVISOR from a site-B-only member.                                                                   | `hr.admin.manage` is an organization-scoped permission. The general authorization evaluator deliberately applies warehouse membership boundaries only to warehouse-scoped permissions. HR-001 limits attendance review, but the role-management policy is not explicit. Confirm whether granting this organization-scoped capability should also authorize global HR role administration; do not call this an established tenant leak or arbitrary role escalation. |
| KIRO-06 | Workflow policy question                               | Certifying a complete start/end correction for an open shift closes its effective interval and later clock-out is refused.                                | HR-031 intentionally replaces effective attendance with the certified pair, and HR-021 permits at most one pair. The probe proposes a past end time while describing the worker as still working, so the resulting completed interval follows the submitted proposal. Decide whether current open shifts should disallow full-pair certification or need a clearer warning.                                                                                         |
| KIRO-09 | Search UX risk under an explicit inference policy      | On 13 Oct 2026, a yearless 1–15 Oct range resolves to 2025 and lookup returns NO_MATCH when only a 2026 draft exists.                                     | The parser documents and tests the latest-past-occurrence policy. Inferred matches require a choice, not silent navigation. The NO_MATCH branch does not show a year clarification when there is no match, so clearer feedback is useful. Changing to the current containing range is a policy change, not a confirmed parser violation.                                                                                                                            |

These five cases belong in a separate ranked policy/risk list with the observed facts and possible impact, not in the confirmed-bug totals. A decision is needed before turning their diagnostic expectations into permanent regressions.

## Consolidation scope

Keep the 16 findings from `kiro-triage.json`, add the five accepted Kiro findings above, merge KIRO-08 into ROOT-01, and retain the five policy/risk cases separately. Result: 21 distinct confirmed defects, of which 20 have runtime/component/browser evidence and one is a source-based deduction. Use the final Kiro ranking as the authoritative priority list; priorities inside the individual research reports are provisional.
