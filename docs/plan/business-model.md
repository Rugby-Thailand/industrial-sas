# Business model

## Customer

Thai mid-market manufacturers with 1–3 warehouses and 500–5,000 SKUs.

Exclude large-enterprise custom deployments and micro-sites that need only a stock list.

## Value

- Faster receiving.
- Fewer stock errors.
- Audit-ready history.
- Controlled order-to-factory release.
- Thai-first adoption.

## Product

Multi-tenant B2B SaaS. Each organization owns its users, warehouses, data, policy, and entitlements.

## Launch

- Sales-led pilot.
- One factory first.
- Manual contract and billing.
- Server-enforced entitlements.
- Pricing and self-service signup: open decisions.

## Cost

Track cost per tenant:

- Database reads, writes, indexes, and storage.
- File storage and bandwidth.
- Jobs, exports, and observability.
- Support and onboarding.

Set limits only from measured pilot use.

## Compliance

Treat customer, employee, supplier, and audit data as protected. Thai legal counsel must confirm PDPA, retention, residency, export, and breach duties before launch.

## Expansion

Add outbound, planning, automation, or billing only after the pilot proves demand and unit cost.
