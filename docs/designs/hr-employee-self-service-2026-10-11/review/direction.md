# Direction contract and review boundaries

User direction: keep the supplied hrzoft references friendly and rounded, change their colors to match the existing application, design one reusable component in 2–4 images, then design all pages, produce an HTML preview and full plan, have Kiro review/fix, and complete a final preview review for the user.

Selected under that instruction: Action Card (four studies) and ten workflow boards. They are critique/composition references, not user-approved production designs. No new visual identity was requested. Exact tokens and readable synthetic copy take precedence over imagegen text and pixel inaccuracies.

Quality bar: the mobile first view contains Today, recorded punches and the next action; shortcuts are recognizable labelled cards; desktop shell stays familiar. List → detail → form → decision is coherent. Amounts/counts reconcile, pending/returned/certified statuses have words and shapes, originals remain visible. Primary touch targets are at least 48px. Focus, dark theme, reduced motion, loading/empty/error/denied, draft recovery and sensible empty states are supported. Avoid decorative gradients, invented policy claims, dead buttons, public pay documents or credential inputs.

Artifacts: preview/index.html, styles.css, app.js; plan.md; images/components and images/pages; manifest.json and prompts. Original reference catalog is ../../references/hr/README.md. Production sources and global tokens are read-only for this task.

Required review evidence: desktop 1440×960 and mobile 390×844, light/dark home, leave, masked payslip, reviewer, import and component showcase screenshots. Gallery includes all boards. First captures are review/initial; final captures will be review/final. Browser results enumerate all 23 routes at both widths. Review normal content at document top with fonts loaded; a fixed bottom navigation appears at the viewport boundary in a full-page mobile capture.

Detector ran once against preview source; six warnings only concern Geist/Geist Mono. They are deliberately retained to match the incumbent system and user instruction. No warning grants permission to redesign the app's fonts.

The final reviewer may find material preview defects. It must name disposition (ship/fix/rebuild/recapture), evidence, material findings, scored dimensions and scope/limitations. A fix verdict concerns listed changes only. Review is about the standalone design preview, not backend implementation, authentication or statutory policy validation.

Documenter boundary: this is an ordinary extension of the current visual system. Compare the final CSS/tokens against src/app/globals.css, preserve incumbent design files, and report evidence in review/documentation-review.md. Do not create root design tokens or migrate the production app. All application code remains outside the write boundary.
