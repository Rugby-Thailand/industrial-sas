# Thailand HR policy evidence — 2026-10-11

Verified 12 October 2026; filename retains the implementation session date. Research for the configurable Thailand preset. This evidence covers ordinary adult private-sector employees under the Labour Protection Act. It does not establish defaults for civil servants, state enterprises, minors or occupations governed by special rules. Applicability must be recorded before activating a company policy.

## Currency and counting limits

Act No. 9 was published on 7 November 2025 and became effective on 7 December 2025. Use the new 120/60 maternity figures. Several general Ministry summaries still display obsolete 90/45 or 98/45 figures; those older maternity and personal-business descriptions do not control this preset. The current Department reporting form now includes continued childcare and spouse leave, corroborating the categories. [Senate explanation of commencement](https://www.senate.go.th/view/386/News/Latest/399/TH-TH), [Department form, page 3](https://eservice.labour.go.th/documents/form-doc/ELPSI1001.pdf).

The Gazette PDF text extraction is corrupted; amendment pages 2 and 3 were rendered and read visually. The new spouse and continued-childcare provisions say “days” without expressly saying working days or calendar days. A definitive Department counting interpretation was not found in this research. Do not present a calendar-day basis for either category as a verified legal requirement. Store the counting basis explicitly and resolve it before deploying the relevant deduction engine. A deliberately more generous company choice can be documented separately from the legal baseline.

The unchanged maternity provision counts holidays. Its 120-day entitlement is therefore a calendar-day span, while annual, personal-business and the sick-pay cap are explicitly working-day quantities. Sick leave must not stop being requestable when its paid cap is exhausted. Military leave likewise separates the official call-up duration from the paid cap.

## Implications for the app preset

These are implementation recommendations derived from the distinctions above, not additional statutory claims:

- Prefill a draft with the verified baseline. Require an authorized HR user to review the employee scope, company calendar, counting rules and effective date before activation. An application setup gate must not imply that employee legal rights are suspended.
- Store leave entitlement, paid-day limits, eligibility, counting basis and evidence requirements separately. Do not model every category as a finite balance.
- An annual monthly earning rate is a company choice. The law does not require the earlier illustrative 12 days or 1 day monthly. A grant available on the first service anniversary is a possible minimum preset; reconcile it with the company's leave-year model so eligibility never loses the floor.
- Leave expiry, carryover, cancellation and termination settlement need explicit company rules and separate legal review. Do not automatically erase unused statutory annual leave on 31 December or deduct approved-but-cancelled leave twice.
- Select and publish each company's actual holiday calendar. A government or banking holiday list is a candidate source, not automatic proof of a private company's required calendar.
- Supervisor routing, announcement audiences, notification channels and payslip verification are application/company settings. This research establishes no Thai statutory default for them and no nationwide common-practice claim.

## Evidence boundaries
