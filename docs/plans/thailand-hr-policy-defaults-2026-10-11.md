# Thailand HR default policy preset

Implementation session dated 11 October 2026; legal sources verified 12 October 2026. User direction: provide editable defaults based on Thai legal minimums or suitable local practices, while retaining the policy-dependent workflow gate. The editable draft, immutable activation versions and server validation are now implemented on the final delivery branch; company activation remains an explicit HR action. See [final execution plan](ui-hr-final-execution-2026-10-12.md) for release evidence. Companion: [HR follow-through](hr-production-follow-through-2026-10-11.md).

## What HR sees

Settings starts with a **Thailand — legal minimums** draft. Each setting shows either **Thai legal minimum** or **Suggested company setting**, its unit, eligibility, effective date and source. HR can keep the preset or increase benefits, review the company settings, then choose **Activate policy**. A blank form and invented preview balances are not the starting point.

Build the settings, validation and policy infrastructure with this preset now. Employee writes, publication and sensitive payslip access remain gated independently until the corresponding company policy is active and the supporting workflow is deployed. Saving a draft does not activate it. This application gate does not remove statutory leave rights or replace the company's existing HR process.

Scope: ordinary employees covered by Thailand's Labour Protection Act. HR must confirm coverage and applicable existing contracts/work rules; sector exceptions and more generous existing benefits cannot be overwritten by applying a minimum preset. Sources and amendment precedence are recorded in the [legal evidence](../references/thailand-hr-policy-evidence-2026-10-11.md).

## Statutory starting values

The first implementation must separate leave entitlement from employer-paid entitlement. An exhausted paid allowance must not automatically reject a legally available leave type.

| Setting                               | Thailand preset                                                                           | Calculation and validation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Annual leave                          | At least 6 paid working days per year after one continuous year of service                | First grant on the one-year anniversary; subsequent grants use the employee's service anniversary. Monthly accrual and first-year pro-rata leave are optional company benefits, not mandatory defaults. Validate an alternative calendar-year scheme across the transition so it does not reduce the minimum. |
| Sick leave                            | Actual sickness; employer pays up to 30 working days per year at minimum                  | No 30-day cap on leave itself. Track paid days separately. At 3 or more working days of sick leave, the default requests medical evidence; support an explanation when evidence cannot be supplied. Occupational injury and maternity are not charged to ordinary sick leave.                                 |
| Necessary personal/business leave     | At least 3 paid working days per year                                                     | Available without a one-year waiting period. HR defines the necessary-business categories under company work rules. No invented probation exclusion.                                                                                                                                                          |
| Maternity leave                       | Up to 120 days per pregnancy, including intervening holidays; employer pays up to 60 days | Pregnancy-related examination days count toward the entitlement. Do not deduct annual or sick leave. Employer payment and any separate social-security benefit are distinct; the application does not calculate a social-security claim.                                                                      |
| Leave to assist a spouse giving birth | Up to 15 days per pregnancy, fully employer-paid                                          | Must be exercised within the statutory 90-day period after birth. Use the amended Act's counting rules, not an annual 15-day balance.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Additional newborn care               | Up to 15 additional days at 50% wages for the qualifying newborn condition                | Applies to the eligible employee after maternity leave, with medical certification. Not a general childcare quota for all parents.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Sterilization leave                   | Physician-certified procedure/recovery duration, paid                                     | No invented fixed annual quota.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Military call-up leave                | Statutory call-up duration; employer pays up to 60 days per year                          | Applicable inspection, training or readiness call-up, with official evidence. Paid cap and leave duration remain separate.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Training/development leave            | Qualifying statutory training or examination leave; unpaid by default                     | Apply the statutory notice and permitted refusal rules rather than a hard 30-day entitlement cap. Company-paid training can be configured as an improvement.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Traditional holidays                  | At least 13 days per year, including National Labour Day                                  | HR selects and announces the site's actual calendar and applicable substitute days. Government/bank holiday calendars are suggestions, not an automatic private-company calendar.                                                                                                                             |
| Weekly rest                           | At least 1 day per week, normally no more than 6 days apart                               | Preserve employee/site schedules. No global assumption that Saturday and Sunday are always nonworking days; exceptions need a separately reviewed rule.                                                                                                                                                       |

Annual, sick, sterilization, military and holiday foundations are supported by [Ministry of Labour employee rights](https://www.mol.go.th/employee/สิทธิหน้าที่นายจ้าง-ลูกจ้าง) and the current Act sections cited in the legal evidence. That summary contains old maternity/personal-leave wording. The personal-business 3-day entitlement and payment come from [Act No. 7, sections 34 and 57/1](https://www.mol.go.th/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2019/07/labour_2541_7.pdf). The maternity, spouse and newborn-care changes took effect on 7 December 2025: [Act No. 9](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/89818.pdf), [government amendment announcement](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/48/iid/452044).

The amended spouse and continued-newborn-care provisions use “days” without explicitly stating working or calendar days. This research did not find a definitive Department interpretation. For a concrete protective starting configuration, offer **15 scheduled working days** for each qualifying event as a suggested company benefit, not as a claim about the statute's unit. Preserve the spouse's statutory 90-day exercise window; do not invent a requirement that every granted day be completed before day 90. A narrower counting configuration requires verified legal interpretation before activation. Spouse entitlement is per pregnancy; twins do not create two quotas.

## Suggested company settings

These values are product recommendations for HR review, not claims that Thai law requires them or that every Thai employer uses them.

| Setting                     | Prefilled recommendation                                                                                            | Guardrail                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Annual leave granting       | 6 working days on each service anniversary, starting after 1 year                                                   | HR may raise the allowance, enable first-year grants or choose a validated accrual scheme. Applying this preset cannot replace a higher existing allowance.                                                 |
| Sick/personal paid year     | January–December, Asia/Bangkok                                                                                      | Anniversary annual-leave year remains separate; use the employee's real schedule to determine working days.                                                                                                 |
| Annual carryover/expiry     | Preserve unused granted days; no automatic forfeiture                                                               | This is a protective company default, not a statutory unlimited-carryover claim. Record the agreed carryover arrangement and any later expiry rule explicitly; retained balances are never silently zeroed. |
| Request units               | Whole scheduled working days for annual/personal leave initially                                                    | Partial days require a defined schedule and smallest-unit policy. Calendar-day statutory types use their own counting rules.                                                                                |
| Reviewer                    | Assigned supervisor; authorized HR fallback when no eligible supervisor exists                                      | No self-review. Statutory notification/evidence types must not inherit a discretionary annual-leave veto or an advance-notice requirement that defeats their rights.                                        |
| Annual/personal reservation | Reserve at submission; no negative ordinary allowance by default                                                    | Reserve and consume atomically. Statutory leave/payment-cap handling is type-specific, not a blanket insufficient-balance rejection.                                                                        |
| Pending cancellation        | Employee may withdraw an undecided request                                                                          | Release a reservation exactly once and retain the history.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Approved cancellation       | Employee may request cancellation before leave starts; reviewer confirms                                            | Do not restore used days merely because the employee clicks Cancel. Started leave goes to HR for an audited adjustment; closed attendance periods follow revision rules.                                    |
| Announcements               | HR publishers; current organization's active employees selected by default; optional authorized site/group audience | Drafts remain private. Show audience/count for confirmation before explicit publication. Never include another organization.                                                                                |
| Announcement reading        | Ordinary read receipts; formal acknowledgment off; unpinned, publish immediately when HR chooses Publish            | Edits create a new version; substantive republishing becomes unread for the audience. Optional scheduling uses Asia/Bangkok.                                                                                |
| Notifications               | In-app request decisions and published announcements                                                                | Deduplicated events. Email/SMS delivery is off until separately configured.                                                                                                                                 |
| Payslip viewing             | Own published statements only; fresh account verification required for reveal and download                          | Verify through the supported identity provider, enforced on the server. If that integration cannot satisfy the policy, sensitive access stays disabled.                                                     |
| Verification duration       | 5 minutes, editable within supported provider limits                                                                | Expiry is server-enforced. Hide, navigation, logout and period/version changes clear displayed sensitive values. No custom salary PIN or client-only authorization.                                         |
| Download                    | Authorized private delivery; at most 60-second issuance lifetime where supported                                    | URL/access design must match actual storage capability. Record download intent; no salary payloads in logs.                                                                                                 |
| Payslip publication         | Explicit HR publication; immutable versions; explicit audited replacements                                          | Source format, employee mapping and retention need real payroll evidence. No default parser or guessed deletion interval.                                                                                   |

## Implementation contract

1. Add a pure, versioned preset (`TH_LPA_MINIMUM_2026_10_11`) containing source references, legal effective dates, rule kind, units, eligibility and pay-cap distinction. Keep operational recommendations in separate fields. No live employee or holiday records are seeded by choosing a preset.
2. Add per-organization, per-capability policy drafts and immutable effective versions. Server resolves organization/actor and enforces HR configuration permission. Leave versions also record covered employee/site categories. Schema must represent unlimited/condition-based leave rather than overloading a numeric balance.
3. Reuse existing Settings fields, shared select/input/status primitives, Thai/English localization and `useHrWrite` outcome handling. Show the minimum beside editable values. Client and server validation reject below-minimum settings for covered employees, disabling statutory types, invalid eligibility, conflicting effective dates and schemes that undergrant. Higher contractual rights require preservation and explicit transition review.
4. Separate **Save draft** from **Activate**. Activation validates applicable coverage, actual work calendars, holiday selection, eligible reviewer routing, imported opening-balance reconciliation and required provider/storage capability. Gate each workflow independently; a missing payslip integration cannot disable attendance or announcements.
5. Record activation actor/time/source preset/revision/effective date through the existing audit/idempotency contracts. Preserve optimistic version checks. A network retry must not create another policy version or duplicate grants.
6. Pin policy, schedule, holiday and evidence snapshots to every accepted request. A policy update creates a future effective version; it does not recalculate old leave or rewrite a closed period. Existing employee balances come from a verified migration/import, not from presumed zero usage of the default entitlement.
7. Enforce gates inside write/sensitive-read APIs as well as navigation. Return an actionable `POLICY_NOT_ACTIVE` or capability-unavailable outcome; avoid fake success and do not merely hide a menu.
8. Add legal-boundary and domain tests: annual 5 rejected/6 accepted; first-year optional grant versus statutory anniversary grant; monthly-accrual transitions do not undergrant; sick leave beyond 30 accepted with exhausted employer-pay allowance; current maternity 120/60 rather than 98/45; spouse birth-window boundaries; conditional newborn care; working/calendar-day distinctions; holiday substitution; statutory type cannot be disabled; preservation of higher existing benefits and unused annual days; draft cannot authorize writes; cross-tenant denial; activation retry; policy revisions preserve historical charges; expired payslip verification blocks both APIs.

## Simple example for the product

An employee has completed one year. The draft starts with **6 annual-leave days**. HR changes that to **10**, reviews the other settings and activates the policy from its effective date. A configured value of **5** is rejected for covered employees. If the employee has used 2 days and has 1 day pending, the ledger shows **10 granted − 2 used − 1 reserved = 7 available**. Opening usage is reconciled before activation; clicking a preset does not grant an existing employee a fictitious clean balance.

The earlier example of 12 days per year / 1 day per month was illustrative. It is not this statutory preset. Likewise, announcement audiences and five-minute payslip verification are editable recommendations rather than legal leave minimums.
