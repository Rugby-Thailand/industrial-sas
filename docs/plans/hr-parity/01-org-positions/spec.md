# S01 — Company, org structure, positions and reporting line

> Historical research baseline: “What we have today” and task IDs below describe the pre-implementation investigation, not current coverage. Current behavior and limitations are maintained in `docs/operations/hr-foundation.md`.

## Summary

HRZoft models a multi-company tenant with a 7-step company profile (name/EN, 13-digit tax ID, Thai address, bank accounts, contact, logo), branches that carry a Revenue-Department branch number and a Social-Security employer account, and per-employee **department (แผนก)**, **position (ตำแหน่ง)**, **level (ระดับ, e.g. CEO)** and **supervisor (หัวหน้างาน)** on an "Employment info" tab.
This run reached the authenticated admin demo and directly inspected company info, branches, the General/Data-management settings tabs, and the employee "Employment info" editor and filters.
Our app has no company profile, no departments, no positions and no position levels; it stores a per-person `supervisorUserId` and uses **warehouses as branches** (code/name/status only) and as the employee's site.
Biggest gaps: a company profile (tax/bank/address) feeding payroll/tax docs; branch tax/SSO codes; a department master; a position + level master; and approval routing driven by level/supervisor.
No team ("ทีม") sub-level or configurable document-numbering screen was found in the demo.
Counts: **14 seen, 9 guess, 2 unknown; 25 tasks** (S01-20 is DUP → S06-13).

## What we have today

Read boundaries: `convex/schema.ts` (`hrEmployees`, `warehouses`), `convex/hr/setup.ts` (`saveEmployee`), `src/features/hr/EmployeeForm.tsx`, `EmployeesScreen.tsx`, `SettingsScreen.tsx`, `HrSiteSelect.tsx`; context map `docs/plans/hr-core-flow-2026-10-18/index.html` NODES `org`, `pos`.

- `warehouses` (used as branches/sites) carry only `code`, `name`, `status`. No address, Revenue branch number, SSO employer account, EN name, branch type, department, team or company profile exists anywhere in the schema (searched: zero hits for department/positionLevel/jobPosition/documentNumber/runningNumber/companyProfile/taxId/branchCode).
- `hrEmployees` stores `warehouseId` (site), `code` (free text, regex `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._-]{0,31}$`), `displayName`, optional `userId`, optional `supervisorUserId`, reviewer/excluded-reviewer lists, employment start/end, `status` (`ACTIVE`/`INACTIVE`), optional weekly `schedule`, and audit/version fields. There is no department, position, level, probation state, employment type, or workplace field.
- `EmployeeForm.tsx` already edits code, name, **site** (`HrSiteSelect`), status, linked account, **supervisor** (member picker, cannot equal the linked account), start/end dates and the weekly schedule. `saveEmployee` validates the supervisor is an active member and enforces one-employee-per-account plus site scope. So per-person reporting line and a branch/site reference exist; department/position/level, branch tax codes and the company profile do not.
- `SettingsScreen.tsx` has Holidays, Access (roles) and Policy panels only — no Company, Branches, Department or Position panels; these are proposed entry points and must pass the back-navigation contract when built.

## UX notes (proposed for our app; Thai labels are HRZoft's observed copy)

- **Company profile** (new Settings panel/route): a labelled form grouped as `ข้อมูลบริษัท` / `ข้อมูลที่อยู่` / `ข้อมูลบัญชี` / `ข้อมูลการติดต่อ`. Fields: name, name (EN), tax ID (13 digits), address + address (EN), province/district/sub-district/postcode, repeatable bank-account rows (bank + account number, add/remove), phone, fax, logo upload. HRZoft presents it as a 7-step wizard (`3/7` done); we do not need to copy the wizard, only the data.
- **Branches**: a list with `เพิ่มสาขา` and an add/edit form carrying Revenue-Dept branch number (`เลขที่สาขา กรมสรรพากร`, head office = `00000`), SSO employer account (`เลขที่บัญชีนายจ้าง ประกันสังคม`), Thai address, EN name, a branch type and an active toggle. Today our `warehouses` only hold code/name/status; extend rather than replace, since employees reference the site.
- **Employee "Employment info" tab**: department (`แผนก`, required), position (`ตำแหน่ง`, required), level (`ระดับ`), supervisor (`หัวหน้างาน`), branch (`สาขา`), workplace (`สถานที่ทำงาน`), employment status/type with probation. In our `EmployeeForm.tsx`, site + supervisor already exist; department/position/level/type/probation are new. Keep readable labels; a position dropdown should list the shared master, not free text.
- **Hierarchy**: a manager's view can be derived as "responsible departments (D)" and "subordinates (S)" — mirror these as filters/columns once positions and the reporting line exist.
- **Empty states**: propose `No departments yet`, `No positions yet`, `No branches yet` rather than blank selects; not separately tested.
- **Out-of-scope labels seen but owned elsewhere**: `บทบาท` (role: Owner/Staff/Administrator) is slice 02; employee personal/payroll/leave tabs are slice 03.
