# ลูกค้า / Customers · Yongkit domain and UX research

**Decision.** Give each customer one record per Yongkit organization. A warehouse worker can save a job ticket with the name on the paper. An admin can link that ticket to the customer later. One customer can have several registered establishments and delivery sites. This lets staff answer “Which jobs and products belong to this customer, and where do we deliver?” before sales or accounting exists.

**Starting point.** The scanner is instructed to copy ชื่อลูกค้า character for character. The saved ticket has an optional customer string, and the product has a separate free-text `customerReference`. Add explicit links alongside both. A product reference might be a customer’s part number, so matching its text to a name is insufficient evidence of ownership. **Thai tax rules that affect the design**

- Section 86/4 lists the purchaser’s name and address on a full tax invoice. For a VAT-registered buyer, the Revenue Department also calls for its TIN and the establishment on its VAT registration certificate (ภ.พ.20). It permits head-office wording or `00000`, and branch wording with its registered number or the five-digit code. These obligations apply at invoicing. They give no reason to require a TIN to save a warehouse customer today. [Revenue Code §86/4](https://www.rd.go.th/english/37741.html) · [RD Notification 199, buyer clauses 7 and 9](https://www.rd.go.th/27982.html) · [RD clarification on VAT-registered buyers](https://www.rd.go.th/49981.html)
- For entered Thai TINs, accept Thai or ASCII digits and pasted spaces/hyphens, then store 13 ASCII digits. Reading from the left, multiply digits 1–12 by weights 13, 12, …, 2. Sum the products. Digit 13 must equal `(11 − (sum mod 11)) mod 10`. For remainders 0 and 1 the expected digits are 1 and 0 respectively. Passing confirms a checksum only. RD operates a separate service for checking issuance. Allow saving without a TIN while staff confirm a mistyped number. [Thailand TIN country sheet, §II](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/thailand-tin.pdf) · [Company-number validator](https://github.com/arthurdejong/python-stdnum/blob/master/stdnum/th/moa.py) · [Check-digit implementation](https://github.com/arthurdejong/python-stdnum/blob/master/stdnum/th/pin.py) · [RD TIN service](https://www.rd.go.th/42533.html)
- Offer an existing customer when an entered TIN already matches. Within that customer, identify a registered establishment by TIN **and** branch code. The same taxpayer can have `00000` and `00001`, and either establishment can receive deliveries at an unregistered dock. Ask which site the ticket means when it matters.

## Matching names from Thai job tickets

| Example input (illustrative)                       | Recommended comparison and outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `บริษัท สยามกล่อง จำกัด` · `บจก.สยามกล่อง`         | Recognize bounded legal prefixes/suffixes, including the joined Thai spelling. Derive comparable stem “สยามกล่อง” and form “private limited”. Suggest the record. Preserve the legal name and both source spellings.                                                                                                                                     |
| `สยามกล่อง จำกัด (มหาชน)` · `บมจ.สยามกล่อง`        | Parse the public-company form first. Retain it as a distinguishing value. A conflicting private-company form requires review. [DBD naming guidance](https://www.dbd.go.th/download/downloads/03_boj/detail_name_reservation.pdf) · [NSO legal-form abbreviations](https://www.nso.go.th/nsoweb/storage/title_presentation/2024/20240429123205_14009.pdf) |
| `SIAM BOX CO., LTD.` · `Siam Box Company Limited`  | Case-fold Latin letters and recognize terminal legal-form variants. Preserve words such as “Box” and site numbers. Link a Thai counterpart only through a known English name or an approved alias.                                                                                                                                                       |
| `สยาม กล่อง` · `สยามกล่องฯ` · `สยามกล่อง โรงงาน 2` | An additional space-insensitive search can surface candidates. Treat ฯ as possible omitted text and retain site qualifiers. Keep vowels, tone marks, digits and meaningful punctuation. Shared stems can lead to different customers.                                                                                                                    |

- **Keep two comparison levels.** For exact lookup use Unicode NFC, trim and collapse whitespace and fold Latin case. Use legal-form expansion, compact spacing and OCR similarity only to retrieve suggestions. Show the actual name and a reason, e.g. “ตรงกับชื่ออื่นที่ยืนยันแล้ว / Matches a confirmed alias”. [Unicode normalization](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/)
- **Approve links and aliases separately.** Default “เชื่อมโยงเฉพาะใบนี้ / Link this ticket only”. Offer “จำชื่อนี้สำหรับใบถัดไป / Remember this name for future tickets” to an admin. Store the original alias, comparison key, organization, customer, optional site, example ticket, approver and time. Reuse only a unique, active, exact approved alias with compatible context. A shared alias, conflicting site, archived customer or OCR near-match goes to review. Retiring an alias affects future matching. A reviewer can correct an individual past link.

## Anti-patterns

- Generating a master for every OCR spelling, making a scan wait for customer creation, or overwriting the text on historical tickets during cleanup.
- Stripping มหาชน, ฯ or every space and merging by the result. Treating one confirmed ticket link as approval to remap past and future tickets.
- Inferring a tax branch from a delivery address, defaulting an unknown establishment to `00000`, or storing identifiers as integers.
- Filling the phone’s first screen with tax fields, displaying “0 jobs” during a failed read, or exposing contact details in every search result.
- Adding balances, invoices or credit controls to make a new customer record look complete.

## Recommended v1 scope

- **Warehouse staff** can find customers by the names they see on paper, open linked products and recent jobs, and identify the receiving site. A missing match never prevents a ticket save.
- **Organization admins** can create a named customer with a generated code, maintain optional legal names, TIN, establishments, addresses and contacts, confirm links and aliases, resolve ambiguous names, merge with an audit trail, and archive or restore.
- **First acceptance cases** are a name-only Thai customer, an English-only name, one TIN with head office and branch plus a third delivery site, a shared nickname that stays unresolved, an archived alias, a product reference that is a part number, and Back after a failed edit on a 390px phone.
- **Later**: invoicing, credit and payment terms, tax determination, financial reporting and automated registry verification.
